FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses worldwide, and schedule calls and support in your time zone.

We're new to AI. Where should we start?

Start with a free consultation or an AI Discovery Sprint. We'll identify where AI can save time or grow revenue, and show you a working prototype before you commit to a full build.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After the consultation you get a clear, fixed proposal — no surprises. Small automations can be very affordable; larger platforms are scoped in phases.

Is our data secure?

Your data stays in secure, access-controlled cloud infrastructure, is used only for your solution, and is never used to train public models. We're happy to sign an NDA.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. On full payment, the code, models configuration and deliverables built for you are yours.
